## Separately proposed finite-difference follow-up — not this correction's gate

This section is a design proposal for a subsequent policy freeze, not authorization to change the existing FD test, its threshold or its flags. The fixed diagnostic already found truncation dominant in all 11 failures; shrinking h eventually increases float32 arithmetic error. It is therefore inappropriate to pick whichever observed h passes.

A principled assessment can separate three claims:

- **Computational derivative parity:** keep the existing full JVP/reverse-AD comparisons with the independent quantized-input float64 oracle and all existing tolerances. This checks the formal learned computation, not a derivative of the float32 quantization staircase or of the physical plant.
- **Local finite-difference consistency:** use every case/direction and the entire fixed grid `h={2^-7,2^-8,2^-9,2^-10}` in the independent smooth float64 oracle. With central quotients D_j, form all three Richardson estimates `R_j=(4*D_(j+1)-D_j)/3`. Predeclare the finest estimate R_2, never the best observed one. Require both R_2 versus oracle AD and R_2 versus R_1 to satisfy the existing x64 FD allowance `2e-8 + 2e-5*abs(oracle_AD)`; report all quotients/estimates and differences. This is a convergence-and-consistency check, not a rigorous Taylor remainder bound. Failure remains failure/inconclusive, with no extended grid or alternate selected step.
- **Float32 finite response:** evaluate independent64 predictions at the exact achieved float32 plus/minus endpoint inputs, keeping the fixed h roster and full original parameter/norm precision charged to the public path. Compare each endpoint forecast under the existing normalized forecast allowance. A scalar finite-response allowance follows by propagating those endpoint allowances through the fixed output coefficients and division by `2*h`; do not invent a separately tuned derivative tolerance or subtract the truncation term from the old failed gate. This tests resolvable finite response and arithmetic, separately from local AD.

Any such replacement is an explicit prospective policy decision informed by known diagnostic evidence, never a newly discovered passing old qualification. Its final details and harness must be committed before execution. No passing outcome is asserted here, and no physical sensitivity accuracy follows from passing these numerical checks.
